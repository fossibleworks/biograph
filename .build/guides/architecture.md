This is a single Frappe app (`healthcare/`) plus one Vue SPA (`patient_portal/`).

- `healthcare/hooks.py` is the wiring hub. It holds:
  - `doc_events`: a `*` wildcard for medical-record history, plus Sales Invoice, Payment Entry, Company and Patient.
  - `scheduler_events`: appointment reminders, daily status and fee-validity updates.
  - `override_doctype_class`: Sales Invoice → `HealthcareSalesInvoice`.
  - `doctype_js`, `standard_queries`, portal menu items and jinja methods.
- `healthcare/healthcare/` is the main module:
  - `doctype/<snake_name>/` (139 doctypes). Each holds `<name>.json` (schema), `<name>.py` (controller), `<name>.js` (form script), optional `_list.js`/`_calendar.js`, and `test_<name>.py`.
  - `custom_doctype/` extends ERPNext doctypes (sales_invoice, payment_entry).
  - `api/patient_portal.py` holds the `@frappe.whitelist()` endpoints the portal calls.
  - `report/`, `page/`, `print_format/`, `web_form/`, `workspace/`, `dashboard_chart*/`, `number_card/` and `setup/` hold the remaining Desk features.
  - `utils.py` holds shared invoice and billing helpers that hooks call.
- `healthcare/controllers/` holds shared controllers (e.g. `service_request_controller.py`, `queries.py`).
- `healthcare/regional/india/` holds the ABDM integration.
- `healthcare/patches/v0_0|v15_0|v16_0/` holds data migrations, registered in `healthcare/patches.txt`.
- `healthcare/www/` and `healthcare/templates/` hold portal pages. `patient_portal.html` is generated by the Vite build.
- `healthcare/public/js` holds Desk JS bundled into `healthcare.bundle.js`.
- `patient_portal/` (Vue) builds into `healthcare/public/...` and talks to the backend through frappe-ui resources calling whitelisted methods in `healthcare.healthcare.api.patient_portal`. It also uses Socket.IO realtime (`src/socket.js`).
- `healthcare/tests/` holds the shared test bootstrap (`HealthcareTestSuite`, which extends ERPNext's `ERPNextTestSuite`).

Dependency direction: `healthcare` → `erpnext` → `frappe`. Healthcare reaches into ERPNext billing (Sales Invoice, Payment Entry, Company) through hooks and overrides, not by editing ERPNext.
