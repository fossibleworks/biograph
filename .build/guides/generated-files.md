Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them with their tools instead.

- **`healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*`**: committed Vite build output of the Patient Portal (hashed `patient_portal-<hash>.js/.css/.map`). Regenerate it with `yarn build`. Pre-commit excludes it.
- **`healthcare/public/dist/`**: Frappe asset build output. It is excluded from pre-commit, prettier and semgrep.
- **`patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts`, `patient_portal/components.d.ts`**: generated by unplugin (frappe-ui vite plugin) and headed `/* eslint-disable */ // Generated by unplugin-vue-components`.
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`**: translation template, regenerated weekly by `generate-pot-file.yml` (`.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh`). The `*.po` files come from Crowdin, which opens `fix: sync translations from crowdin` PRs.
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` version string**: bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc` prepareCmd). The fork keeps its own version.
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock`. Update it through yarn only.
- **Doctype `*.json`**: produced by the Frappe desk (DocType editor). Edits should keep `fields`/`field_order` consistent. During upstream sync they are merged as a 3-way union.
- **`.secrets.baseline`**: maintained with the detect-secrets tool.
- **Ignored by git:** `*.pyc`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `dist/`, `node_modules/`, `healthcare/docs/current`.
