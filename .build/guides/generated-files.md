Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them from source, or leave them alone.

- **`healthcare/public/frontend/`**: built Patient Portal assets with hashed `assets/patient_portal-*.js/.css/.map`. They are tracked in git. Regenerate them with `yarn build` from the `patient_portal/` source. Pre-commit excludes this path.
- **`healthcare/public/dist/`** and `dist/`: Frappe asset build output. `dist/` is gitignored and pre-commit excludes `healthcare/public/dist/`.
- **`healthcare/public/node_modules`**: a tracked link into node_modules. `node_modules/` itself is gitignored.
- **`yarn.lock`**: the Yarn lockfile. Update it only through `yarn`.
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`**: translatable-string template. The weekly `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow regenerates it, and Crowdin produces the `.po` files and opens PRs titled `fix: sync translations from crowdin`.
- **`patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts`, `patient_portal/components.d.ts`**: generated by the frappe-ui/Vite auto-import plugins.
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` version string**: semantic-release rewrites it on release branches.
- **`.secrets.baseline`**: detect-secrets baseline. Update it only with `detect-secrets scan --baseline`.
- **`healthcare/docs/current`**: gitignored generated docs.
- **`__pycache__/`, `*.pyc`, `*.egg-info`, `.ruff_cache`**: caches, which are ignored.

**DocType `.json` files are not generated in the usual sense.** Frappe writes them when a DocType is saved in developer mode, and they are the source of truth for the schema. Edit them with care and keep the `fields` and `field_order` lists consistent.
