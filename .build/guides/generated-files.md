Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them instead.

- **Portal build output:** `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*` (hashed `patient_portal-<hash>.js/.css/.map`, committed) and `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html`. Both are produced by `yarn build` in `patient_portal/`. Pre-commit excludes `healthcare/public/dist/` and `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/`.
- **Type stubs:** `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts` are generated by the frappe-ui/Vite plugins.
- **Translations:** `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated by a scheduled workflow (`update_pot_file.sh`). The `.po` files come from Crowdin.
- **Version string:** semantic-release rewrites the version in `healthcare/__init__.py` (`chore(release): Bumped to Version …`).
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock`. Update it only through yarn.
- **Secrets baseline:** `.secrets.baseline` is maintained by detect-secrets.
- **Doctype JSON:** `doctype/*/*.json` is written by Frappe's DocType editor. Hand edits are allowed, but keep the `fields` and `field_order` keys consistent (the upstream-sync policy uses a 3-way union).
- **Ignored:** `*.pyc`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `dist/`, `node_modules/`, `healthcare/docs/current`, `.ruff_cache`.
