Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them instead.

- **Portal build output**:
  - `healthcare/public/frontend/` is tracked and contains hashed assets such as `patient_portal-5b0abb31.js` and `.css`.
  - `healthcare/public/patient_portal/` is Vite's `outDir`.
  - `dist/` is gitignored.
  - Change `patient_portal/src` and rebuild with `yarn build`. pre-commit already excludes `healthcare/public/dist/` and `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/`.
- **Auto-generated type stubs**: `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts` come from the frappe-ui Vite plugin.
- **Lockfiles**: `yarn.lock`. Update it with yarn, never by hand.
- **Translation template**: `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated by the weekly `generate-pot-file` workflow. Translations come from Crowdin.
- **Version string**: `healthcare/__init__.py` `__version__` is rewritten by semantic-release (`.releaserc`).
- **DocType JSON**:
  - `doctype/*/<name>.json` is normally produced by Frappe's DocType editor. Hand edits are fine but must keep JSON valid and keep `fields` and `field_order` consistent.
  - The `class ... # begin: auto-generated types` blocks in controllers are generated by Frappe.
- **Ignored**: `node_modules/`, `__pycache__/`, `*.pyc`, `*.egg-info`, `healthcare/docs/current`, `.ruff_cache/`.
