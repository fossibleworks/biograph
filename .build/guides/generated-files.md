Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them instead.

- **`healthcare/public/frontend/`**: built Patient Portal assets (hashed `assets/patient_portal-*.js/.css/.map`, `index.html`), produced by `vite build`. They are committed, so rebuild them from `patient_portal/` sources.
- **`healthcare/public/dist/`** and **`dist/`**: build output. Gitignored and excluded from linters.
- **`node_modules/`**, including `healthcare/public/node_modules`: dependencies. Gitignored.
- **`patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts`** and **`patient_portal/components.d.ts`**: type stubs generated by the frappe-ui/Vite plugins.
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`**: translation template, regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow (`.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh`).
- **`yarn.lock`**: change it only through yarn.
- **`.secrets.baseline`**: maintained by `detect-secrets`.
- **DocType `.json` files** are normally exported by Frappe when a doctype is saved in developer mode. Edit them carefully: keep `fields` and `field_order` in sync, and expect the `modified` timestamp to change.
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` version string**: bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc` prepareCmd). Do not bump it by hand.
- Also gitignored: `*.pyc`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `healthcare/docs/current`.
