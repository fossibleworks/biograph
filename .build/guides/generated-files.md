Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them with their tool.

- **Patient Portal build output:**
  - Tracked hashed bundles in `healthcare/public/frontend/` (`assets/patient_portal-<hash>.js|.css|.map`, `index.html`, `manifest.json`).
  - `vite.config.js` targets `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets` and writes the HTML entry to `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html`.
  - Rebuild with `yarn build`; never patch the bundles. Pre-commit already excludes `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/` and `healthcare/public/dist/`.
- **`patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts`:** type declarations generated by the frappe-ui/Vite plugins.
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`:** regenerated weekly by `generate-pot-file.yml` through `.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh`. Crowdin manages translations.
- **`yarn.lock`:** lockfile; change it only through yarn.
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` version:** bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc` prepareCmd) on stable branches.
- **Doctype `*.json`:** written by Frappe's DocType editor and exported. When editing by hand, keep the `fields`/`field_order` structure and bump `modified`.
- **Ignored:** `dist/`, `node_modules/`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `healthcare/docs/current`. Also local-only: `.ruff_cache/`, `.env`, `.goals/`, `.tasks/`.
- **`.secrets.baseline`:** detect-secrets baseline; update it with `detect-secrets scan --baseline`.
