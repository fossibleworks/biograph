Do not hand-edit these:

- **Portal build output.** `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/patient_portal-<hash>.js|.css|.js.map` are hashed Vite bundles and are committed. `patient_portal/vite.config.js` also writes to `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets`. To change them, edit `patient_portal/src/**` and run `yarn build`.
- **`healthcare/public/dist/`** is a Frappe asset build. Pre-commit excludes it.
- **`node_modules/`** and `healthcare/public/node_modules` (a symlink to the bench's node_modules) are gitignored and local only.
- **Lockfiles.** Update `yarn.lock` only through yarn, never by hand.
- **`patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts`** are type stubs that the frappe-ui/Vite plugins generate automatically.
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`** is regenerated weekly by `generate-pot-file.yml`. The `*.po` files come from Crowdin sync PRs.
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` `__version__`** is bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc` prepareCmd).
- **`.secrets.baseline`** is maintained by detect-secrets.
- **Gitignored:** `*.pyc`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `dist/`, `node_modules/`, `healthcare/docs/current`, and `.ruff_cache`.

The **doctype `.json` files** are generated by Frappe's DocType editor, but they are the committed source of truth for schema. Edit them carefully, keeping the order of `fields` and `field_order` in sync. During upstream syncs, merge them as a 3-way union of `fields`/`field_order`.
