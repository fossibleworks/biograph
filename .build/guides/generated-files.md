Do not hand-edit these:

- **`healthcare/public/frontend/**`** is the committed Vite build of the patient portal (hashed `assets/patient_portal-*.js/.css`). Change `patient_portal/src` and rebuild instead. Pre-commit excludes it.
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`** is regenerated by `.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh` and a weekly workflow.
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` `__version__`** is bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc` prepareCmd). Don't bump it by hand.
- **`patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts`, `patient_portal/components.d.ts`** are generated by Vite plugins.
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock`. Update it with yarn, never by hand.
- **DocType `*.json`** files are maintained through Frappe's DocType editor/export. When editing by hand, keep `fields` and `field_order` consistent.
- **Ignored:** `dist/`, `node_modules/`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `healthcare/docs/current`, `.ruff_cache`.
- **`AGENTS.md`, `.claude/rules/build-rules.md`, `.cursor/rules/`, `.github/instructions/build-rules.instructions.md`** are tool mirrors managed by Build. Edit `.build/RULES.md` / `.build/guides/` instead.
- **`.secrets.baseline`** is the detect-secrets baseline. Update it with `detect-secrets scan`.
