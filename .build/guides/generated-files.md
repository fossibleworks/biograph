# Generated and vendored files

Do not hand-edit these files:

- **Patient portal build output**:
  - `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*` (hashed `patient_portal-*.js`, `.js.map`, `.css`), `healthcare/public/frontend/index.html` and `manifest.json` are committed build artifacts.
  - The Vite config targets `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets` and rewrites `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html`.
  - Change `patient_portal/src` and rebuild with `yarn build` instead.
- `healthcare/public/node_modules`: a committed link entry, not source.
- `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts`: generated by the frappe-ui/Vite auto-import plugins.
- `healthcare/locale/main.pot`: regenerated weekly by `generate-pot-file.yml`. The `*.po` translations come from Crowdin (`crowdin.yml`).
- `yarn.lock`: lockfile, updated only through yarn.
- `healthcare/__init__.py` version string: bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc`).
- DocType `*.json` files: normally produced by the Frappe DocType editor in developer mode. Hand edits must stay consistent with the Frappe schema.
- Ignored by git: `dist/`, `node_modules/`, `*.egg-info`, `__pycache__/`, `healthcare/docs/current`.
- `.secrets.baseline`: maintained by `detect-secrets`.
