Do not hand-edit these files:

- **Build output:** `dist/`, `healthcare/public/dist/`, `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/` and `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets` (Vite output from `patient_portal/`). `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html` is written by the frappe-ui vite plugin (`indexHtmlPath`).
- **Dependencies:** `node_modules/` (including `healthcare/public/node_modules`) is gitignored. `yarn.lock` is the lockfile; update it with yarn, not by hand.
- **Type declarations:** `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts` are generated by unplugin tooling.
- **Translations:** `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow (`.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh`).
- **Version:** `healthcare/__init__.py`'s version string is rewritten by semantic-release (`.releaserc`).
- **DocType JSON** (`doctype/*/*.json`) is normally generated by the Frappe desk DocType editor. If you edit it by hand, keep `fields` and `field_order` consistent.
- **Auto-generated type blocks** (`# begin: auto-generated types`) in controllers are maintained by Frappe.
- Also ignored: `__pycache__/`, `*.pyc`, `*.egg-info`, `healthcare/docs/current`, `.ruff_cache`.
- `.secrets.baseline` is managed by detect-secrets.
