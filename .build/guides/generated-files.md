Do not hand-edit these generated, vendored or managed paths:

- **Build output:**
  - `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets/` (Vite build output)
  - `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html` (written by the frappe-ui vite plugin)
  - `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/` and `healthcare/public/dist/`
  - `dist/`
  - All of these are excluded from linting.
- **Dependencies:** `node_modules/` (including `healthcare/public/node_modules`) and `yarn.lock`. Update the lockfile only through yarn.
- **Type stubs:** `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts` are auto-generated by the unplugin auto-import and components plugins.
- **Translations:** `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file` workflow. `*.po` files arrive from Crowdin.
- **Version:** `healthcare/__init__.py` holds the version string, which semantic-release bumps.
- **Managed blocks:**
  - `.build/RULES.md`, `.claude/rules/build-rules.md`, `.github/instructions/build-rules.instructions.md`
  - the BUILD GUIDES block in `AGENTS.md` and the ibuild block in `CLAUDE.md`
  - These are managed by Interactor Build. Edit the source guide instead.
- **Local, not committed:** `.goals/`, `.tasks/`, `.env`, `.ruff_cache/`, `__pycache__/`.
- **DocType JSON** (`doctype/*/*.json`) is generated by the Frappe desk form builder but is committed and reviewed. Upstream merges use a 3-way union of `fields` and `field_order`.
