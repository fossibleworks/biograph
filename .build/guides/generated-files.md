Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them with their tool instead.

- **Patient Portal build output:** `healthcare/public/frontend/` (hashed `assets/patient_portal-*.js|css|.map`, `index.html`, `manifest.json`) and `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html` are produced by `vite build` in `patient_portal/`. Edit `patient_portal/src` and rebuild.
- **Type stubs:** `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts` are generated by unplugin (frappe-ui Vite plugin). Both carry `eslint-disable` / `@ts-nocheck` headers.
- **Translations:** `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file` workflow.
- **Version string:** `healthcare/__init__.py`'s version is bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc`) on release branches.
- **Doctype JSON** (`doctype/*/*.json`) is exported by Frappe when you save in the desk DocType editor. Editing by hand is accepted (the upstream sync policy 3-way-unions `fields`/`field_order`), but keep the JSON shape Frappe would export.
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock`.
- **Secrets baseline:** `.secrets.baseline` is maintained by detect-secrets.
- **Ignored:** `node_modules/` (including `healthcare/public/node_modules`), `dist/`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `healthcare/docs/current`, `.ruff_cache`.
- **Lint exemption list:** `.pre-commit-config.yaml` and `.semgrepignore` hold a very long explicit file list of legacy files exempted from linting. Do not add new files to them to dodge lint.
