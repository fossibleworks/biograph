Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them with the tool that produced them.

- **Patient portal build output**
  - Built assets under `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/` (hashed `patient_portal-*.js`, `.css`, `.map`). These are committed.
  - The Vite output target `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets`.
  - The generated SPA shell `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html` (frappe-ui `indexHtmlPath`).
  - Rebuild with `yarn build`.
- **`patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `components.d.ts`** are generated by the frappe-ui / unplugin Vite plugins.
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`** is regenerated by the scheduled `generate-pot-file` workflow. Add new strings through `_()` / `__()`, not by editing the POT file.
- **`healthcare/public/dist/`**, `node_modules/`, `dist/`, `*.egg-info`, `__pycache__/` and `healthcare/docs/current` are build artifacts, mostly gitignored.
- **Lockfile**: `yarn.lock` at the root, managed by yarn.
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` `__version__`** is bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc`) on version branches.
- **`.secrets.baseline`** is maintained by `detect-secrets`.
- **DocType `.json` files** are normally edited through the Frappe DocType UI and exported. Keep the field order and the `modified` timestamp consistent with a Frappe export.
- `.github/instructions/build-rules.instructions.md` is generated from `.build/RULES.md`. The `AGENTS.md` guides block is managed by Build.
