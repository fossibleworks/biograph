Do not hand-edit these:

- **`healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets/`** and **`healthcare/www/patient_portal.html`** are Vite build output from `patient_portal/` (`emptyOutDir: true`). Edit the sources in `patient_portal/src` and rebuild.
- **`healthcare/public/dist/`** and **`healthcare/public/frontend/assets/`** are bundle output and are excluded from linters.
- **`patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts`** and **`patient_portal/components.d.ts`** are generated by the frappe-ui/unplugin tooling.
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`** is regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow. **`*.po`** files are synced from Crowdin (`crowdin.yml`). Do not edit either by hand.
- **`healthcare/__init__.py`** holds `__version__`, which semantic-release rewrites (`.releaserc` prepareCmd).
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock`. Update it only through yarn.
- **`node_modules/`**, `dist/`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info` and `healthcare/docs/current` are gitignored.
- **`.secrets.baseline`** is the detect-secrets baseline. Update it with `detect-secrets scan --baseline`.
- **DocType `.json`** files are written by the Frappe DocType editor. Hand edits are allowed, but keep the `fields` / `field_order` consistency. Upstream merges use a 3-way union.
