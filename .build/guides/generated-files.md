Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them with the tool that produced them.

- **`healthcare/public/frontend/`** holds the built Patient Portal bundle (hashed `assets/patient_portal-*.js`, `.css`, `.map` and `index.html`). It is committed. Regenerate it with `yarn build`.
- **`healthcare/www/patient_portal.html`** is written by the frappe-ui Vite plugin (`indexHtmlPath`) during the portal build.
- **`patient_portal/components.d.ts` and `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts`** are generated by unplugin-vue-components and auto-import (header: `// Generated by unplugin-vue-components`).
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`** is regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow through `.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh`.
- **Lockfile:** `yarn.lock`. Change it only through yarn.
- **DocType JSON** (`healthcare/healthcare/doctype/*/*.json`) is normally edited through Frappe's DocType UI or by careful JSON edits. Keep `fields` and `field_order` consistent. During upstream syncs, merge them as a 3-way union.
- **`# begin: auto-generated types` blocks** in controllers are written by Frappe's type generator.
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` `__version__`** is rewritten by semantic-release (`.releaserc` prepareCmd).
- **Ignored files:** `*.pyc`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `dist/`, `node_modules/`, `healthcare/docs/current`. The local `.ruff_cache/` is also generated output.
- **`.secrets.baseline`** is the detect-secrets baseline. Update it with the detect-secrets tool, never by hand.
