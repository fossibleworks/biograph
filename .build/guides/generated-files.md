Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them instead.

- **Patient portal build output:**
  - `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*` (hashed `patient_portal-*.js/.css` and `.map` files)
  - `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html`, written by the frappe-ui Vite plugin (`indexHtmlPath`)
  - Rebuild with `yarn build`.
- `healthcare/public/dist/`, `dist/`, `node_modules/`, `*.egg-info`, `__pycache__/`: build artefacts, gitignored or excluded from linters.
- `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts`: generated by the frappe-ui/unplugin tooling.
- `healthcare/locale/main.pot`: regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow. Crowdin manages translations (`crowdin.yml`).
- `healthcare/__init__.py` `__version__`: bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc` prepareCmd). Do not bump it by hand.
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock` (root workspaces). Update it only through yarn.
- **DocType `.json` files** are written by Frappe's DocType editor. Edit them carefully and keep `fields`/`field_order` consistent. The auto-generated typing block (`# begin: auto-generated types`) inside controllers is produced by Frappe.
- `.secrets.baseline`: maintained by `detect-secrets`.
- `.github/instructions/build-rules.instructions.md` and the Guides block in `AGENTS.md`: generated by Build from `.build/RULES.md`.
