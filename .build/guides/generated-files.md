Do not hand-edit these paths. Regenerate them with the owning tool instead.

- **Patient Portal build output:** `healthcare/public/frontend/**` (hashed `assets/patient_portal-*.js|css|map`, `index.html`, `manifest.json`) is committed build output. `vite.config.js` also writes to `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets` and generates `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html` (`indexHtmlPath`). Change `patient_portal/src` and run `yarn build`.
- **`healthcare/public/dist/`** and **`healthcare/public/node_modules`** are bundle and dependency output. Pre-commit and prettier exclude them.
- **Translations:** `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow (`.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh`). Crowdin syncs the `.po` files (`crowdin.yml`), with commits titled `fix: <lang> translations`.
- **Version string:** `healthcare/__init__.py`'s `__version__` is rewritten by semantic-release (`.releaserc` prepareCmd). Don't bump it by hand on release branches.
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock` (root workspace). Update it only through yarn.
- **Type stubs:** `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts` are generated by the frappe-ui/unplugin tooling.
- **Doctype JSON** (`doctype/*/*.json`) is normally saved from the Frappe desk (developer mode). When editing it by hand, keep `field_order` and `fields` consistent and keep the `modified` timestamp.
- **Ignored:** `*.pyc`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `dist/`, `node_modules/`, `healthcare/docs/current`.
- **Secrets baseline:** `.secrets.baseline` is maintained by `detect-secrets`.
