Do not hand-edit these files:

- **Patient Portal build output**
  - `healthcare/public/frontend/` is committed. It holds hashed bundles in `assets/patient_portal-<hash>.js/.css`, plus `.map`, `index.html`, `manifest.json` and `favicon.png`.
  - `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html` is the index that the frappe-ui Vite plugin writes (`indexHtmlPath`).
  - Vite's `outDir` is `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets`.
  - To change any of these, edit `patient_portal/src/` and rebuild with `yarn build`.
- **`healthcare/public/dist/`**: Frappe bundle output. It is excluded from pre-commit and prettier.
- **Lockfiles**: `yarn.lock`. Change it only through yarn.
- **Translations**
  - `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated weekly by `generate-pot-file.yml` through `.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh`.
  - Crowdin syncs translations back in PRs titled "fix: sync translations from crowdin".
- **Version string**: semantic-release rewrites the version in `healthcare/__init__.py` on release branches. Do not bump it by hand.
- **Secrets baseline**: detect-secrets maintains `.secrets.baseline`.
- **Ignored output** (`.gitignore`): `dist/`, `node_modules/`, `__pycache__/`, `*.pyc`, `*.egg-info`, `healthcare/docs/current`.
- **Doctype JSON** (`doctype/*/*.json`): normally generated by the Frappe DocType editor. When editing by hand or merging, keep the `fields` and `field_order` union consistent. That is the upstream-sync policy.
