Do not edit these by hand.

- **Portal build output:** `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets/` and `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html` are produced by `vite build` in `patient_portal/`. Edit `patient_portal/src/` and rebuild instead.
- **Other bundles:** `healthcare/public/dist/` and `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/`. Pre-commit excludes both, and `dist/` is gitignored.
- **Auto-generated type stubs:** `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts` come from the frappe-ui/Vite plugins.
- **Translations:** `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file` workflow (`.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh`). The `*.po` files are synced from Crowdin.
- **Version string:** `healthcare/__init__.py` `__version__` is rewritten by semantic-release (`.releaserc` prepareCmd).
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock`. Update it only through yarn.
- **Secrets baseline:** `.secrets.baseline`. Update it with `detect-secrets`.
- **Ignored:** `node_modules/`, `__pycache__/`, `*.pyc`, `*.egg-info`, `dist/`, `healthcare/docs/current`, `.ruff_cache`.
- **DocType JSON** (`doctype/*/*.json`) is generated by Frappe's DocType editor but is committed source. Keep edits minimal and consistent with `field_order`. During upstream sync, merge `fields` and `field_order` as a 3-way union.
