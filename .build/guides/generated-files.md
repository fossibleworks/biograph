Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them with the tool that owns them.

- `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*` (hashed `patient_portal-*.js/.css/.map`), `healthcare/public/frontend/index.html` and `manifest.json` are **built Patient Portal output** committed to git. Change `patient_portal/src` and rebuild. `healthcare/public/dist/` is also build output and is excluded from linters.
- `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts` are type stubs generated by the frappe-ui/Vite plugins.
- `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated by `.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh` (weekly workflow). `.po` translations come from Crowdin PRs (`fix: sync translations from crowdin`).
- `yarn.lock` is the lockfile. Update it only through yarn.
- `.secrets.baseline` is the detect-secrets baseline. Update it with `detect-secrets scan --baseline`.
- `healthcare/__init__.py` holds `__version__`, which semantic-release bumps on release branches. Do not bump it by hand.
- DocType `.json` files under `doctype/*/` are normally written by Frappe's DocType editor (`bench --site … console` or the desk in developer mode). Edit them carefully and keep the field ordering Frappe emits.
- Ignored (never commit): `node_modules/`, `dist/`, `*.egg-info`, `__pycache__/`, `healthcare/docs/current`, `.venv/`.
