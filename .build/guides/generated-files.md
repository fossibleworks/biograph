Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them through their source instead.

- **`healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*`** (hashed `patient_portal-*.js`, `.js.map`, `.css`) plus `healthcare/public/frontend/index.html` and `manifest.json` are **Vite build output** of `patient_portal/`. Change `patient_portal/src` and rebuild. They are excluded from pre-commit.
- **`healthcare/public/node_modules`** is a committed **symlink** to a developer's local bench path. Treat it as an artifact; do not depend on it or modify it.
- **`patient_portal/components.d.ts`, `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts`** are auto-generated by the frappe-ui/unplugin tooling.
- **`yarn.lock`** is a lockfile, changed only through yarn.
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`** is the translation template. A scheduled workflow regenerates it (`update_pot_file.sh`). `.po` files come from Crowdin PRs.
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` `__version__`** is bumped by semantic-release on the `version-*` branches. Don't bump it by hand.
- **Doctype `*.json`** files are exported by Frappe when a doctype is saved in Desk (developer mode). Keep edits consistent with that export format.
- Ignored outputs (`.gitignore`): `dist/`, `node_modules/`, `*.egg-info`, `__pycache__/`, `healthcare/docs/current`. `patient_portal/.gitignore` also ignores `dist`, `dist-ssr` and `*.local`.
- `.secrets.baseline` is the detect-secrets baseline. Update it with the tool, not by hand.
