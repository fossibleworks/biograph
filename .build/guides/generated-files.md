Do not hand-edit these files.

- **Patient Portal build output:** `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*` contains hashed `patient_portal-*.js`, `.js.map` and `.css` files that are committed to the repo. pre-commit and prettier exclude them along with `healthcare/public/dist/`. Regenerate them with `yarn build`; do not edit them.
  - `patient_portal/vite.config.js` currently sets `outDir` to `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets`. That is not the committed `frontend/assets` path, so check where the build actually writes before committing rebuilt assets.
  - The Vite plugin also rewrites `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html` (`indexHtmlPath`).
- **frappe-ui type stubs:** `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts` are generated by the frappe-ui/Vite plugins.
- **Translations:** `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow. The `.po` files come from Crowdin (`crowdin.yml`, PR title `fix: sync translations from crowdin`).
- **Version string:** `healthcare/__init__.py` is bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc` prepareCmd).
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock`.
- **`healthcare/public/node_modules`:** a node_modules entry is committed under `healthcare/public`. Treat it as vendored.
- **DocType JSON** (`doctype/*/*.json`) is written by Frappe's DocType editor. Edit it carefully. In upstream merges, take the 3-way union of `fields` and `field_order`.
- **Ignored:** `*.pyc`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `dist/`, `node_modules/`, `healthcare/docs/current`. Local untracked dirs `.goals/`, `.tasks/` and `.env` belong to the tooling and must not be committed.
- `.secrets.baseline` is maintained by detect-secrets.
