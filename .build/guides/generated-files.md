Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them from their source instead.

- **`healthcare/public/frontend/**`** (hashed `assets/patient_portal-*.js/.css/.map`, `index.html`, `manifest.json`): these are built patient-portal assets and are committed. Change `patient_portal/src` and rebuild.
- **`healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets`** and **`healthcare/www/patient_portal.html`**: these are Vite build output paths (see `vite.config.js` `outDir` and `indexHtmlPath`).
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`**: the translation template, regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow through `.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh`.
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` version string**: semantic-release bumps it on release branches (`.releaserc`). Do not change it by hand.
- **Lockfiles**: `yarn.lock` is managed by Yarn.
- **`patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts`, `patient_portal/components.d.ts`**: type stubs generated by the frappe-ui / unplugin tooling.
- **`.secrets.baseline`**: maintained by `detect-secrets` (update it with `detect-secrets scan --baseline`).
- **Ignored outright** (`.gitignore`): `dist/`, `node_modules/`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `healthcare/docs/current`.
- **Bench-managed DocType JSON** (`doctype/*/*.json`): Desk's DocType editor usually writes these. Hand edits must keep valid Frappe schema, including `field_order` matching `fields`.
- Pre-commit already excludes `healthcare/public/dist/`, `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/` and `node_modules`.
