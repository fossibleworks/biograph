Do not hand-edit these files. Regenerate them instead.

- **Patient portal build output.** Vite writes compiled bundles (hashed `patient_portal-<hash>.js/.css/.map`) and `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html` (frappe-ui `indexHtmlPath`). Bundles currently committed under `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/` are build artifacts. Rebuild with `yarn build` and never edit them. Note that `vite.config.js` points `outDir` at `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets`, so check where a rebuild actually lands before you commit it.
- `healthcare/public/dist/` and `dist/` are ignored, and pre-commit/prettier exclude them.
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`** is regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow. The `*.po` translations arrive from **Crowdin** PRs (`fix: sync translations from crowdin`).
- **`patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts`, `components.d.ts`** are generated by the frappe-ui/unplugin Vite plugins.
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` version string** is bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc`) on `version-*` branches. Do not bump it by hand on feature work.
- **Doctype `.json` files** are written by the Frappe DocType editor (`bench --site ... ` + Desk, with developer_mode on). Hand edits are possible but must stay valid Frappe schema. During upstream syncs, merge `fields`/`field_order` as a 3-way union.
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock` is maintained by yarn.
- `.secrets.baseline` is maintained by `detect-secrets`.
- Ignored by `.gitignore`: `*.pyc`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `node_modules/`, `dist/`, `healthcare/docs/current`.
