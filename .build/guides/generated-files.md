Do not edit these by hand:

- **`healthcare/public/frontend/`** (`assets/patient_portal-<hash>.js/.css/.map`, `index.html`) holds the committed Vite build output of `patient_portal/`. Regenerate it with `yarn build`.
- **`healthcare/www/patient_portal.html`** is written by the frappe-ui Vite plugin (`indexHtmlPath`).
- **`patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts`** and **`components.d.ts`** are generated by the frappe-ui/Vite plugins.
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`** is regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow (`.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh`).
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` version** is bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc` prepareCmd).
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock`.
- **Untracked:** `node_modules/` (also `healthcare/public/node_modules`), `dist/`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, and `healthcare/docs/current`, all ignored via `.gitignore`.
- **Managed by Build:** `.github/instructions/build-rules.instructions.md` and `.claude/rules/build-rules.md` are produced from `.build/RULES.md`. The `BUILD GUIDES` block in `AGENTS.md` is managed the same way. Edit the source, not the copies.
- **`.secrets.baseline`** is the detect-secrets baseline. Update it only through `detect-secrets scan --baseline`.
