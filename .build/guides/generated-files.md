Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them instead.

- **Portal build output:** `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*` (hashed `patient_portal-*.js`, `.css` and `.map`) and the generated portal index `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html`. Vite writes these from `patient_portal/` (`yarn build`). They are committed, so rebuild them after changing portal source.
- **DocType JSON** (`healthcare/healthcare/doctype/*/*.json` and other `*.json` metadata such as workspace, report, number_card and print_format). These are normally edited through Frappe Desk (developer mode) and exported. If you edit them by hand, keep `field_order` and `fields` consistent. On upstream syncs, merge them as a 3-way union.
- **Translations:** `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated weekly by `generate-pot-file.yml` (`.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh`). The `.po` files come from Crowdin.
- **Version:** semantic-release rewrites `healthcare/__init__.py` `__version__`.
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock`, and `.secrets.baseline` (the detect-secrets baseline).
- **Ignored:** `node_modules/`, `dist/`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `healthcare/docs/current`, `.ruff_cache`.
- `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `components.d.ts` are generated by the frappe-ui and Vite plugins.
