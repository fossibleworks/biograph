Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them instead:

- **Built patient portal assets:** `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/patient_portal-*.js|.css|.js.map`, `healthcare/public/frontend/index.html`, and the Vite-written `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html`. These come from `patient_portal/` with `yarn build`. The Vite outDir is `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets`. Pre-commit excludes `healthcare/public/dist/` and `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/`.
- **Type stubs:** `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts` are generated by the frappe-ui/unplugin Vite plugins.
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock` (root). Update it only through yarn.
- **Translations:** `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated weekly by `generate-pot-file.yml` (`.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh`). `.po` files come from Crowdin.
- **Version string:** `healthcare/__init__.py` `__version__` is bumped by semantic-release, not by hand.
- **DocType JSON** (`doctype/*/*.json`, reports, workspaces, number cards, charts) is exported by Frappe when you save in developer mode. Edit it through the desk UI or keep it consistent with what Frappe would export.
- **Symlink:** `healthcare/public/node_modules` is a committed symlink to a local bench path. Ignore it and don't commit changes to it.
- **Ignored:** `node_modules/`, `dist/`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `healthcare/docs/current`, `.venv/` (untracked).
- **Baselines:** `.secrets.baseline` (detect-secrets) and `.semgrepignore`.
