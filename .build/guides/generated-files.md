Do not hand-edit these files. Regenerate them instead.

- **`healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*`** (hashed `patient_portal-<hash>.js/.css/.js.map`), `healthcare/public/frontend/index.html` and `manifest.json` are build output of the Vue portal and are committed. Lint (pre-commit) excludes `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/`. To change them, edit `patient_portal/src` and rebuild.
- **`healthcare/www/patient_portal.html`** is written by the frappe-ui Vite plugin (`indexHtmlPath`).
- **`healthcare/public/dist/`** and `dist/` are build output and are git-ignored.
- **`patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts`** and **`components.d.ts`** are auto-generated type shims.
- **`healthcare/public/node_modules`** is a tracked symlink/stub. Never commit real `node_modules/`.
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock` (root workspace). Update it only through yarn.
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`** is generated by `.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh` and the weekly workflow. Crowdin produces the `*.po` files.
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` `__version__`** is bumped by semantic-release. Do not bump it manually.
- **Doctype `.json` files** are normally exported by Frappe when a doctype is saved in developer mode. Keep the field order and the `modified` timestamps consistent with that export.
- **`.secrets.baseline`** is maintained by detect-secrets.
