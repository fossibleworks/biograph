Do not hand-edit the following.

- **Patient portal build output:**
  - `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*` (hashed `patient_portal-*.js/.css/.map`, committed)
  - `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets`
  - `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html`
  
  All of these are written by `vite build`. Regenerate them with `yarn build`. Pre-commit excludes `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/` and `healthcare/public/dist/`.
- **Typings generated by frappe-ui/unplugin:** `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts`.
- **Translations:** `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow. Translated `.po` files come through Crowdin.
- **Version string:** `healthcare/__init__.py` `__version__` is bumped by semantic-release. Never bump it by hand. On upstream syncs, keep the fork's `.releaserc`.
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock`. Change it only through yarn.
- **Doctype JSON** (`doctype/*/*.json`) is exported by Frappe's DocType editor. Edit it with care and keep `fields`/`field_order` consistent.
- **Ignored:** `node_modules/`, `dist/`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `healthcare/docs/current`, `.ruff_cache`.
- `.secrets.baseline` is maintained by detect-secrets.
