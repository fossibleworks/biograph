Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them instead.

- **Patient portal build output.** `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*` contains hashed `patient_portal-<hash>.js/.css/.map`, and it is committed. `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html` is written by the frappe-ui Vite plugin (`indexHtmlPath`). Edit `patient_portal/src` and rebuild with `yarn build`. Note that `vite.config.js` sets `outDir` to `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets`, so check where the build actually writes before committing.
- `healthcare/public/dist/`: Frappe asset bundle output. It is excluded from pre-commit and prettier.
- `dist/`, `node_modules/`, `*.egg-info`, `__pycache__/`, `healthcare/docs/current` are gitignored.
- `healthcare/public/node_modules` is a tracked link. Leave it alone.
- **Lockfile:** `yarn.lock`. Update it only through yarn.
- **Translations:** `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow. The `*.po` files are synced from Crowdin (`crowdin.yml`). Do not hand-edit either.
- `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `components.d.ts` are generated by Vite plugins.
- **Doctype JSON** (`doctype/*/*.json`) is normally produced by Frappe's DocType editor. You may edit it by hand, but keep `field_order` and `fields` consistent and bump `modified`.
- `.secrets.baseline`: maintained by detect-secrets.
- **Version string** in `healthcare/__init__.py`: semantic-release rewrites it on stable branches.
