Do not hand-edit these paths. Regenerate them from their source.

- **`healthcare/public/frontend/`** (`assets/patient_portal-<hash>.js/.css/.map`, `index.html`): built Patient Portal output. It is committed. Change `patient_portal/src` and rebuild. Pre-commit excludes `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/`.
- **`healthcare/public/dist/`**, **`dist/`**: build output. Gitignored and excluded from pre-commit.
- **`node_modules/`** (root, `patient_portal/`, `healthcare/public/node_modules`): gitignored.
- **`patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts`, `patient_portal/components.d.ts`**: auto-generated by the frappe-ui/unplugin Vite tooling.
- **`yarn.lock`**: lockfile, changed only by yarn.
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`**: regenerated weekly by `generate-pot-file.yml`. `.po` translations come from Crowdin (`crowdin.yml`).
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` version string**: bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc`).
- **DocType `.json` files**: written by Frappe's DocType editor (developer mode). Hand edits are allowed, but keep `fields` and `field_order` consistent.
- **`.secrets.baseline`**: managed by detect-secrets.
- `*.pyc`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `.ruff_cache/`: gitignored.
