Do not hand-edit these paths:

- **Build output:** `dist/`, `healthcare/public/dist/`, `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/`, and `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets/` (the Vite outDir for `patient_portal`). Rebuild them with `yarn build`.
- **Dependencies:** `node_modules/` (including `healthcare/public/node_modules`) and `__pycache__/`, `*.pyc`, `*.egg-info`. All are gitignored.
- **Lockfile:** `yarn.lock`. Update it only through yarn.
- **Generated docs:** `healthcare/docs/current` (gitignored).
- **Translations:** `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is generated by `.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh` and the `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow. The `*.po` files come from Crowdin (`crowdin.yml`).
- **Version string:** `healthcare/__init__.py` is bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc` prepareCmd).
- **Secrets baseline:** `.secrets.baseline` is maintained by detect-secrets.
- **Frappe-generated DocType scaffolding:** the `<doctype>.json` schemas are exported by Frappe when a DocType is saved in Desk. Edit them carefully. Upstream syncs merge `fields` and `field_order` as a three-way union.
- **Engine and agent state:** `.goals/`, `.tasks/`, `.ruff_cache/`, and the managed block in `AGENTS.md` (rendered by Build).
