Do not hand-edit these:
- **Built Patient Portal assets:** `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*` (hashed `patient_portal-*.js/.css/.map`, committed to git), `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets` (the Vite `outDir`), and `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html` (written by frappe-ui's vite `buildConfig.indexHtmlPath`). Regenerate them with `yarn build`.
- **Type stubs:** `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts`.
- **`healthcare/public/dist/`**, `dist/`, `node_modules/` and `healthcare/public/node_modules` are ignored or excluded build output.
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock`. Update it only through yarn.
- **Translations:** `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated by the weekly `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow. The `.po` files come from Crowdin.
- **Version string** in `healthcare/__init__.py` is bumped by semantic-release.
- **`.github/instructions/build-rules.instructions.md`** and the managed block in `AGENTS.md` are generated by Build from `.build/RULES.md`. Edit the source file instead.
- `.secrets.baseline` is managed by detect-secrets.
- `healthcare/docs/current` is gitignored.

Doctype `.json` files are generated by the Frappe DocType editor but are kept under source control. Edit them carefully and keep `fields` and `field_order` consistent.
