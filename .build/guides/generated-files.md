Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them instead.

- `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*.js|*.css|*.map` and `healthcare/public/frontend/index.html`: **committed build output** of the Vue portal. Change `patient_portal/src` and rebuild.
- `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts`: generated by the frappe-ui/Vite plugins.
- `healthcare/locale/main.pot`: regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file` workflow. The `*.po` files are synced from Crowdin (`fix: <lang> translations` PRs).
- `yarn.lock`: managed by Yarn. Update it only through `yarn`.
- `healthcare/__init__.py` `__version__`: bumped by semantic-release (`chore(release): Bumped to Version …`).
- Ignored outputs (never commit them): `dist/`, `node_modules/` (including `healthcare/public/node_modules`), `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `healthcare/docs/current`, `.ruff_cache`.
- `healthcare/public/dist/` is excluded from Semgrep.
- `.secrets.baseline` is maintained by `detect-secrets`.
- DocType `.json` files are written by Frappe's DocType editor (`bench migrate` / Desk). When merging, combine `fields`/`field_order` as a union rather than rewriting them by hand.
