# Generated and vendored files

Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them instead.

- **`healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*`** holds hashed patient-portal bundles (`patient_portal-<hash>.js/.css/.js.map`). They are committed build output from `patient_portal/`. Rebuild with `yarn build`. Pre-commit and prettier exclude them.
- **`healthcare/www/patient_portal.html`** is written by the frappe-ui vite plugin (`indexHtmlPath`).
- **`healthcare/public/dist/`** is Frappe bundle output (excluded from pre-commit).
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`** is regenerated weekly by `generate-pot-file.yml` (`.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh`). The `.po` translations come from Crowdin (`crowdin.yml`) in PRs titled `fix: sync translations from crowdin`.
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` `__version__`** is bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc` `prepareCmd`). The fork keeps its own version during upstream syncs.
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock`. Update it only through yarn.
- **`node_modules/`, `dist/`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `healthcare/docs/current`** are gitignored. The `.ruff_cache/` directory also appears at the repo root.
- **`patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts`, `patient_portal/components.d.ts`** are generated by the frappe-ui/unplugin auto-import tooling.
- **`.secrets.baseline`** is the detect-secrets baseline. Update it with `detect-secrets scan --baseline`, never by hand.
- **Doctype `*.json` files** are exported by Frappe from the DocType editor. Edits are allowed, but keep `fields` and `field_order` consistent. During upstream syncs, merge them as a 3-way union.
