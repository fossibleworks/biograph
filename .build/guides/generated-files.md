Do not hand-edit these:

- **Portal build output:** `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*` (hashed `patient_portal-<hash>.js/.css/.map`, tracked in git) and `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html`, which Vite writes. Edit `patient_portal/src` and rebuild instead.
- **`healthcare/public/dist/`** and `dist/`: build output (gitignored or excluded from linters).
- **`node_modules/`** (also `healthcare/public/node_modules`): gitignored.
- **Lockfile:** `yarn.lock`. Change it only through yarn.
- **Translations:** `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated weekly by `.github/workflows/generate-pot-file.yml`. `.po` files come from Crowdin.
- **Version:** `healthcare/__init__.py` `__version__` is bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc`).
- **Typed stubs:** `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `components.d.ts` are generated by the frappe-ui/Vite plugins.
- **Build-managed rule mirrors:** `.claude/rules/build-rules.md` and `.github/instructions/build-rules.instructions.md` are generated from `.build/RULES.md`. The `BUILD GUIDES` block in `AGENTS.md` is also managed.
- **Doctype JSON** (`doctype/*/<name>.json`) is normally exported by the Frappe Desk DocType editor. Hand edits must remain valid JSON and keep `fields` and `field_order` consistent.
- **Caches:** `__pycache__/`, `.ruff_cache/`, `*.pyc`, `*.egg-info`.
