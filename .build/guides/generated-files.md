Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them with the tool that owns them.

- **`healthcare/public/frontend/`**: built Patient Portal assets (hashed `patient_portal-*.js`, `.css`, `.map`). They are **committed** to git. Rebuild them from `patient_portal/` with `yarn build`. Vite also writes `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html` (`indexHtmlPath`).
- **`healthcare/public/dist/`** and `dist/`: Frappe build output (ignored or excluded).
- **`node_modules/`** (root, `patient_portal/`, `healthcare/public/node_modules`): ignored.
- **`patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts`, `patient_portal/components.d.ts`**: generated by the frappe-ui/unplugin auto-import tooling.
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`**: translation template. The scheduled `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow regenerates it, via `.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh`. `*.po` files come from Crowdin (`crowdin.yml`).
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` version string**: bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc` `prepareCmd`).
- **Lockfiles**: `yarn.lock`. Change it only through yarn.
- **`.secrets.baseline`**: detect-secrets baseline.
- **DocType JSON** (`doctype/*/*.json`) is normally written by Frappe's DocType editor. When editing by hand, keep `field_order` and `fields` consistent. Upstream-sync merges use a 3-way union of both.
- Ignored per `.gitignore`: `*.pyc`, `__pycache__/`, `*.egg-info`, `healthcare/docs/current`, `.DS_Store`.
