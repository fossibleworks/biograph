Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them instead.

- **Patient portal build output**
  - `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*` (hashed `patient_portal-<hash>.js/.css/.map`) and the generated `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html`, which frappe-ui `buildConfig.indexHtmlPath` writes.
  - These come from `yarn build` in `patient_portal/`. Vite's outDir is configured as `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets`.
  - Some of this output is committed. Rebuild it rather than editing it, and do not lint it: pre-commit excludes `healthcare/public/dist/` and `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/`.
- **`dist/`, `node_modules/`, `*.egg-info`, `__pycache__/`, `healthcare/docs/current`** are git-ignored.
- **`patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts`** are auto-generated by the frappe-ui/unplugin tooling.
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`** is regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow (`.github/helper/update_pot_file.sh`). Crowdin syncs the `.po` translations.
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` `__version__`** is bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc` prepareCmd). Upstream sync keeps the fork's version.
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock`. Update it only through yarn.
- **`.secrets.baseline`** is maintained by detect-secrets.
- **DocType `.json` files** are normally produced by the Frappe Desk DocType editor. When editing them by hand, keep `field_order` and `fields` consistent.
