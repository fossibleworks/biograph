Do **not** hand-edit these paths:

- **Built Patient Portal bundles.** `healthcare/public/frontend/` (hashed `assets/patient_portal-*.js/.css/.map`, `index.html`, `manifest.json`) is committed build output. Vite also writes to `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets` and regenerates `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html`. Change `patient_portal/src/` and rebuild with `yarn build`. Pre-commit excludes `healthcare/public/dist/` and `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/`.
- **`healthcare/public/node_modules`** is a committed link/vendored entry, and `node_modules/` is git-ignored.
- **`dist/`, `*.egg-info`, `__pycache__/`, `*.pyc`, `healthcare/docs/current`** are git-ignored.
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock` is managed by Yarn. Update it only through `yarn install`.
- **`patient_portal/components.d.ts` and `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts`** are generated by the frappe-ui/unplugin auto-import tooling.
- **`healthcare/locale/main.pot`** is regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow.
- **`healthcare/__init__.py` version** is bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc` prepareCmd). The fork keeps its own version during upstream syncs.
- **Doctype `.json` files** are Frappe metadata, normally written by the Desk DocType editor. If you edit them by hand, keep `fields` and `field_order` consistent. The upstream-sync policy is a 3-way union of these lists.
- **`.secrets.baseline`** is maintained by `detect-secrets`.
- **`.ruff_cache/`** is a local tool cache.
