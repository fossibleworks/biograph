Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them, or leave them alone:

- **Portal build output:** `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*` (hashed `patient_portal-<hash>.js/.css/.js.map`) and `healthcare/public/frontend/index.html`. These are committed build artifacts of `patient_portal/`. `vite.config.js` also writes to `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets` and rewrites `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html` (`indexHtmlPath`). Rebuild with `yarn build`; do not edit the output.
- **`healthcare/public/dist/`, `dist/`, `node_modules/`:** gitignored build and dependency output.
- **Lockfile:** `yarn.lock`. Update it only through yarn.
- **Translations:** `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file.yml` workflow. `*.po` files come from Crowdin (`crowdin.yml`).
- **DocType JSON** (`doctype/*/*.json`) is generated by the Frappe DocType editor. Hand edits are acceptable, but keep `fields` and `field_order` consistent. During upstream sync, merge them as a 3-way union.
- **Auto-generated type stubs:** `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts`.
- **Build-managed files:** `.github/instructions/build-rules.instructions.md` (generated from `.build/RULES.md`) and the `BEGIN/END BUILD GUIDES` block in `AGENTS.md`. Edit the source files instead.
- **Version string:** `healthcare/__init__.py` is bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc`).
- **`.secrets.baseline`:** maintained by detect-secrets.
