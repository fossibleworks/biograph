Do not hand-edit these. Regenerate them with the owning tool.

- **Portal build output:** `healthcare/public/frontend/assets/*` (hashed `patient_portal-*.js/.css` and `.map` files, committed) and `healthcare/www/patient_portal.html` (written by Vite's frappe-ui `indexHtmlPath`). Rebuild with `yarn build`. Note that `vite.config.js` targets `healthcare/public/patient_portal/assets`.
- **Ignored build dirs:** `dist/`, `node_modules/`, `*.egg-info`, `__pycache__/`, `healthcare/docs/current`, and `healthcare/public/dist/` (excluded from linting).
- **Type stubs:** `patient_portal/auto-imports.d.ts` and `patient_portal/components.d.ts` are generated by the unplugin auto-import tooling.
- **Lockfiles:** `yarn.lock`. Update it via yarn only.
- **Translations:** `healthcare/locale/main.pot` is regenerated weekly by the `generate-pot-file` workflow.
- **Version string:** `healthcare/__init__.py` is bumped by semantic-release (`.releaserc` prepareCmd).
- **Doctype JSON** (`doctype/*/*.json`) is normally produced by the Frappe DocType editor. When editing it by hand, keep `fields` and `field_order` consistent.
- **Secrets baseline:** `.secrets.baseline` is maintained by detect-secrets.
- `.ruff_cache/` is a local tool cache.
